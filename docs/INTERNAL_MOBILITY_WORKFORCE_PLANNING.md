# SANGGAR Internal Mobility & Workforce Planning OS

## Flow
Organization Need → Workforce Plan → Position Demand → Capability Gap → Internal Mobility Opportunity → Explainable Match → Development → Human Decision → Assignment.

## Core entities
- workforce_plans: planning horizons and workforce strategy.
- workforce_plan_positions: target/current headcount and hiring need per position.
- internal_mobility_opportunities: promotion, transfer, rotation, stretch, succession, and project opportunities.
- internal_mobility_matches: explainable readiness/gap/evidence signals for people and opportunities.
- workforce_capability_snapshots: organization/unit-level capability supply versus required capability.

## Governance
Internal mobility data is organization-scoped and protected by RLS. Recommendations are not employment decisions. AI may rank or explain options, identify gaps, and recommend development actions, but humans remain responsible for promotion, transfer, compensation, termination, and succession decisions.

## Intelligence loop
Workforce Demand → Capability Supply → Gap → Talent Discovery → Development → Mobility → New Assignment → Reassessment.

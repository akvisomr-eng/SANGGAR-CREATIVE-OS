# SANGGAR CREATIVE OS — Compliance & Regulatory OS

## Purpose

SANGGAR must be legally operable in Indonesia and designed for international expansion. There is no single global IT licence that covers every country. Regulatory obligations depend on the entity, service, data, users, sector, and country.

## Indonesia baseline

For a SANGGAR service operating in Indonesia, the platform must maintain a regulatory readiness checklist including:
- legal entity and business licensing
- appropriate KBLI/business classification
- NIB and applicable OSS licensing
- PSE Lingkup Privat registration where required
- Personal Data Protection compliance
- electronic transaction requirements
- consumer protection
- intellectual property
- contracts and terms
- child/minor protection where applicable
- tax/accounting obligations
- sector-specific obligations when SANGGAR enters regulated sectors

## International baseline

Maintain jurisdiction profiles rather than assuming one global approval:
- GDPR/European privacy requirements where applicable
- local privacy/data protection laws
- AI governance requirements
- consumer and e-commerce rules
- employment/payroll rules
- tax rules
- intellectual property
- accessibility
- education-sector requirements where applicable
- cross-border transfer requirements

## International standards strategy

Target management-system readiness and, when commercially justified, independent certification for:
- ISO/IEC 27001 — information security management
- ISO/IEC 27701 — privacy information management
- ISO/IEC 42001 — AI management
- ISO 9001 — quality management
- ISO 22301 — business continuity
- ISO 31000 — risk management guidance
- ISO/IEC 20000-1 — service management where applicable
- ISO/IEC 27018 — cloud privacy controls where applicable

Certification is performed by competent external conformity-assessment/certification bodies. SANGGAR may automate preparation, evidence collection, gap analysis, reminders and workflow, but must not fabricate certification or impersonate an authorized body.

## Regulatory automation

Create a Regulatory Workflow Engine with:
- jurisdiction registry
- regulator registry
- licence/registration registry
- obligation registry
- applicability rules
- required documents
- evidence registry
- submission checklist
- application status
- renewal dates
- regulatory changes
- responsible owner
- legal approval
- audit trail

## Application automation levels

LEVEL 1 — Detect
Automatically determine potentially applicable obligations.

LEVEL 2 — Prepare
Generate forms, declarations, technical descriptions, privacy documents and evidence packs.

LEVEL 3 — Validate
Run completeness, consistency and policy checks.

LEVEL 4 — Submit
Submit through an official API or authorized portal integration when technically and legally permitted.

LEVEL 5 — Human authorization
Require authorized person approval for legal declarations, signatures, payments, attestations and other acts that legally bind the organization.

LEVEL 6 — Track
Monitor application status, correspondence, deadlines and renewal.

LEVEL 7 — Maintain
Detect regulatory changes and trigger reassessment.

## Hard boundary

The system must never:
- forge a signature
- misrepresent an organization
- submit false information
- claim certification before it is granted
- bypass regulator controls
- automatically accept legal liability on behalf of a person without authorization

## Compliance evidence vault

Evidence should be versioned and linked to:
- policy
- control
- requirement
- system component
- owner
- test
- audit
- certification
- regulator submission

This creates a continuous evidence chain:
Requirement → Control → Implementation → Test → Evidence → Review → Submission → Approval.

## Launch gate

SANGGAR cannot be marked globally launch-ready merely because the software works.

Launch readiness requires a jurisdiction matrix and an explicit status for each target market:
- Not applicable
- Research required
- Preparation
- Submission ready
- Submitted
- Approved/registered
- Certified
- Renewal due
- Remediation required

## Operating principle

Legal and compliance are product capabilities, but legal decisions remain accountable to authorized humans and qualified professionals where required.

# SANGGAR Succession & Leadership OS

## Flow
Critical Position → Succession Plan → Successor Candidates → Readiness & Evidence → Leadership Development → Human Review → Succession Coverage.

## Core entities
- succession_plans: critical-role succession strategy and readiness horizon.
- succession_candidates: explainable successor readiness, strengths, gaps, evidence and review state.
- leadership_development_tracks: reusable leadership capability programs tied to competencies.
- leadership_development_enrollments: person-level participation and progress linked to existing development plans.

## Intelligence
SANGGAR can identify critical roles without adequate successor coverage, candidates closest to readiness, leadership competency gaps, development priorities, succession readiness trends, and leadership pipeline health.

## Governance
AI can assist with evidence summarization, gap analysis, readiness explanation and development recommendations. It cannot autonomously nominate, promote, terminate, compensate, or appoint employees. Sensitive/protected traits must not be inferred or used for succession decisions.

## Architecture
The module reuses canonical job_positions, people_profiles, competencies, competency_assessments, development_plans, career_paths, and existing workforce analytics rather than creating duplicate identity or skill systems.

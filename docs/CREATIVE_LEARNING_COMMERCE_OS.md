# SANGGAR Creative Learning & Commerce OS

## Purpose

SANGGAR connects learning outcomes to real creative production and economic opportunity:

**Learn → Practice → Create → Improve → Prove → Portfolio → Match → Publish/Sell → Earn → Learn Again**

The learning system must not be limited to courses. A creator's completed project can become skill evidence, a portfolio item, a Creative Passport record, a marketplace submission package, and eventually a revenue signal.

## Learning domains

The initial commercial learning catalog covers:

1. 3D Asset Creation & Commercialization
2. AR & Spatial Creation
3. Photography & Stock Commercialization
4. Video & Motion Commercialization
5. Graphic Design & Digital Assets
6. Illustration, POD & Merchandising
7. Music, Audio & Licensing
8. Digital Products & Creator Business
9. Creative Freelancing & Client Work
10. Creator Portfolio & Monetization

## Marketplace learning

Marketplace-specific learning is connected to the platform registry. The system should teach creators how to evaluate:

- technical requirements
- metadata requirements
- copyright and IP
- model/property releases
- AI provenance and disclosure
- licensing
- exclusivity
- submission workflow
- payout/fee considerations when verified
- policy freshness

SANGGAR must never promise acceptance by an external marketplace. It provides an evidence-based pre-submission assessment; the external platform retains final acceptance authority.

## Data model

The catalog layer uses:

- `creative_learning_catalog`
- `creative_learning_modules`
- `creative_learning_marketplace_links`

These complement the existing:

- `learning_paths`
- `learning_modules`
- `skills`
- `evidence_records`
- `portfolios`
- `creator_gallery_items`
- `creative_platforms`
- `creative_platform_policies`
- `creative_platform_matches`
- `creative_passports`

The catalog is a reusable curriculum blueprint. User-specific enrollment and progress remain in the existing Learning OS rather than creating a second identity or enrollment system.

## Future expansion

The catalog is intentionally extensible to additional creative monetization channels, including stock, POD, digital products, audio licensing, 3D marketplaces, creator memberships, freelance services, direct client work, and future spatial/immersive marketplaces.

Rules are versioned separately from learning content so marketplace policy changes do not require rewriting the curriculum.
